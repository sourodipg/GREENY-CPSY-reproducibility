# Methods implemented by the extension

## Input and frozen boundary

The extension reads the 24×12 BranchN result directories and compiles a probe that includes the frozen `network_kernels.hpp` and the frozen population-building implementation. The production GREENY executable is not edited.

For each `(offset, seed)`, the probe reconstructs the same `z` vector used by the frozen M kernel, sorts agents by `z`, and derives the exact cyclic neighbour graph. The aggregate edge weight between two agents is the exact number of timesteps on which the frozen `metric_matching()` returns that pair.

## Exact edge weights without replaying agent dynamics

For an even population, `metric_matching()` depends on the parity of the cyclic shift. Shift values differing by two yield the same unordered matching. Therefore the complete aggregate M graph can be reconstructed from the counts of the two shift parities over the 12,000 timesteps; no belief, strain or threshold state is required. This is an exact reconstruction of the contact graph under the frozen contact kernel, not an approximation.

## Graph quantities

For the simple aggregate graph:

- `n_nodes`: number of agents.
- `n_edges`: number of distinct undirected contact pairs.
- `density = 2m/[n(n-1)]`.
- `components`: number of connected components.
- `degree_min`, `degree_max`, `degree_mean`, `degree_sd`: standard unweighted degree statistics.
- `strength_min`, `strength_max`, `strength_mean`, `strength_sd`: incident weighted degree, i.e. sum of contact counts.
- `edge_weight_min`, `edge_weight_max`, `edge_weight_mean`, `edge_weight_cv`: statistics of repeated-contact counts.
- `global_clustering`: standard triangle-based global clustering coefficient on the binary aggregate graph; for the frozen M cycle this should be zero for `N=4000`.

## Trait mixing

For anxiety and avoidance, the unweighted scalar assortativity is computed as the Pearson correlation between endpoint trait values over the undirected edge list. The exposure-weighted form repeats each edge endpoint pair with weight `w_ij`, equivalently applying weighted Pearson correlation to the endpoint pairs.

The extension also reports:

- `mean_abs_dA`: mean `|A_i-A_j|` over edges.
- `mean_abs_dV`: mean `|V_i-V_j|` over edges.
- exposure-weighted versions of both.

These quantities distinguish attribute similarity from contact frequency.

## Relation to BranchN outcomes

For each coordinate realization, the graph metrics are averaged over its 12 seeds. Those realization-level graph summaries are joined to the already-computed BranchN `M_Y-M_D` contrasts. Pearson correlation across the 24 realization means is used only as an exploratory diagnostic. The report explicitly labels this as descriptive because the 24 coordinate realizations are a finite sensitivity sample, not a registered population of networks.

## No emergent-social-network claim

The extension does not infer friendship, attachment, tie formation or preference. The graph is an imposed contact/exposure graph generated by the frozen matching rule. This distinction is essential because the contact schedule is exogenous in the present GREENY implementation.
