## Fixed-point criterion
A release is treated as having reached a fixed point when the manuscript, authoritative results, experiment semantics, and reproducibility checks agree without an unresolved high-impact contradiction. The fixed-point audit verifies file presence, 560-row structure, finite values, key contrast reconstruction, encounter-ledger identity, absence of `frozen` scientific terminology, and presence of the latest key results.

The audit passes. It does not claim that GREENY is clinically validated, that every possible contact topology has been explored, or that a unique causal share has been identified. Those remain explicit boundaries of the paper.

## Known non-final matters
Funding text and author-specific submission metadata remain placeholders where the source manuscript already had them. The short clock audit remains exploratory and is reported as a model-scope limitation rather than promoted to a general time-scale invariance claim.
