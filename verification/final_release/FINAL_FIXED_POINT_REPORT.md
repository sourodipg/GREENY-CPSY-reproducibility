## Interpretation at the fixed point
Recipient-role exchange shows no clear systematic effect on the final correlations. Partner-signal ablation changes dyadic state agreement but does not remove the population-level one-sided-to-two-sided displacement. Halving the update fraction attenuates the displacement but does not reproduce the one-sided condition. Simultaneous-versus-sequential comparison remains small. Therefore the current evidence supports a composite intervention-level interpretation rather than a single-mechanism claim.

## Explicit boundaries
This is not evidence for natural attachment formation, an empirically calibrated social network, a universal reciprocity parameter, or a unique causal mediation pathway. The recurrent-local effect is conditional on its imposed coordinate realization, and the model time scale is a specified modelling convention.
